# Privacy Policy for Sudoku GOAT: Master Quest

**Last Updated:** September 28, 2026

**TecDroid** ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage for our mobile game **Sudoku GOAT: Master Quest** (the "Application").

---

### 1. Offline-First & No Personal Data Collection
- **100% Offline Game Data:** All game progress, campaign stage stars, gamer tags, avatar choices, high scores, personal best solving speeds, and stats are stored entirely locally on your device via encrypted local storage (MMKV).
- We do not require registration, login, email addresses, or phone numbers to play the game.
- We do not operate proprietary backend servers that collect or store your personal identity.

---

### 2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs
The Application integrates with **Google AdMob** to display banner, interstitial, and voluntary rewarded video ads (such as for timer overtime extensions and bonus stage unlocks).

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process device-specific information to serve advertisements, prevent fraudulent clicks, and monitor campaign performance. This information may include:
- **Advertising Identifiers:** Android Advertising ID (GAID).
- **Device & Technical Information:** Device model, operating system version, carrier, and screen dimensions.
- **Diagnostics & Performance:** Ad load times, error logs, and app interaction data.

For further details on how Google processes ad data, please review:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google Uses Information From Sites or Apps That Use Our Services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

---

### 3. European Economic Area (EEA) & UK User Consent (GDPR)
In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Google's EU User Consent Policy, users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK are presented with a Consent Management form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) upon launching the app.

Users can choose whether to allow personalized or non-personalized advertising. You may review or update your consent preferences at any time by opening the **Gamer Hub** in the app and selecting **Ad Privacy & GDPR Choices**.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Our Application is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. All ad requests are flagged with a maximum content rating of **PG** (`MaxAdContentRating.PG`) to ensure family-appropriate ad creatives.

---

### 5. Data Security
Because all personal game data remains exclusively on your device, your data security is tied to your device's security. Deleting or clearing app storage will erase your local game progress.

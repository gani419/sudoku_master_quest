Sudoku GOAT: Master Quest

🐐 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

1. 100% Offline Game Architecture

At TecDroid, we prioritize user privacy. Sudoku GOAT: Master Quest is engineered as an offline-first puzzle game. All your game progress, stars, timer records, campaign completion, and profile statistics remain stored strictly on your local device. We do not operate proprietary backend databases, and we never collect your personal contact information.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The application utilizes Google AdMob to display non-intrusive advertisements (banner, interstitial, and voluntary rewarded video ads). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device-level identifiers to serve relevant ads and safeguard against fraud:

  • Android Advertising ID (GAID)
  • Device Hardware & OS details
  • Ad performance and technical diagnostics

To understand how Google processes ad data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms and Google Partner Data Guidelines.

3. GDPR / European User Consent

For players located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, our app utilizes Google's certified Consent Management Platform (User Messaging Platform) to request explicit consent before delivering advertisements. You can re-open and adjust your consent preferences at any time from within the in-game Gamer Hub ➔ Privacy & Settings.

4. Children’s Privacy

The app is designed for audiences aged 13 and older. All ad requests strictly enforce child-safe PG ratings (MaxAdContentRating.PG) to protect younger audiences.
